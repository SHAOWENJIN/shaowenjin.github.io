Sensor Evaluation

Characterisation of the SLAMTEC RPLIDAR A2M12 LiDAR Unit

Experimental analysis of sensing accuracy, resolution and real-world operating boundaries.

LiDARTesting

CategoryMaster’s Project

RoleIndividual coursework project

Project index06 / 10

Overview

Conducted systematic performance testing of a 2D LiDAR sensor to understand the limitations that matter to robotic navigation and environmental perception.

Work & contribution

  • Measured distance accuracy, spatial resolution, angular resolution, spot size and beam divergence.
  • Analysed the relationship between distance and measurement performance.

Outcome

The source project reports a practical working range of approximately 0.1589 m to 13 m, with best precision between 1 m and 5 m.
