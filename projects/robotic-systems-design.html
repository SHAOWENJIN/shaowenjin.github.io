Robotic Systems Design Project

Led the navigation work package for a six-member autonomous mobile robot project, from SLAM and Nav2 integration to physical robot testing.

ROS2Nav2SLAM

CategoryMaster’s Project

RoleNavigation Work Package Lead · six-member team

Project index01 / 10

Overview

Contributed to an autonomous mobile robot built around the Leo Rover platform. The team integrated mapping, localisation, navigation, perception and system-level testing.

Work & contribution

  • Configured ROS2/Nav2 global and local path planning, motion control, costmaps and navigation behaviours.
  • Integrated SLAM and TF2 transforms with mapping and system-integration teammates.
  • Supported physical robot tests demonstrating autonomous mapping, obstacle avoidance and waypoint navigation.
  • Co-authored the Workplace Charter, Requirements Analysis, Preliminary/Final Design Reviews and Technical Evaluation Video; independently produced the final Technical Demonstration.
  • Participated in WBS, Gantt planning and requirements verification.

Outcome

The physical Leo Rover demonstrated autonomous mapping, obstacle avoidance and waypoint navigation.
