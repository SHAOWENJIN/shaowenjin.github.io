Deep Learning-Based Prediction of Photovoltaic Power Generation

A CNN–LSTM forecasting approach combining temporal features and dimensionality reduction.

CNN–LSTMPCA

CategoryUndergraduate Project

RoleUndergraduate graduation thesis

Project index07 / 10

Overview

Investigated machine-learning methods for photovoltaic power generation forecasting.

Work & contribution

  • Applied PCA for dimensionality reduction.
  • Combined CNN local temporal feature extraction with LSTM sequence modelling.
  • Compared prediction errors against CNN, LSTM and XGBoost baseline models.

Outcome

Recognised as an Outstanding Graduation Thesis; lower MAE and RMSE than the baselines reported in the source portfolio.
