Computer Vision

A Comparative Study of Traditional Computer Vision and Deep Learning in Object Recognition

Compared residual CNNs against SIFT, K-Means, Bag-of-Words and SVM on benchmark datasets.

CNNCIFAR-10SVHN

CategoryMaster’s Project

RoleCognitive Robotics and Computer Vision coursework

Project index05 / 10

Overview

Investigated traditional and deep-learning-based approaches to image recognition under a common evaluation.

Work & contribution

  • Developed residual CNNs with Adam, learning-rate scheduling, global average pooling and Dropout.
  • Implemented a SIFT + K-Means + Bag-of-Words + SVM comparison pipeline.
  • Evaluated classification on CIFAR-10 and SVHN.

Outcome

The source project reports 96.34% accuracy on SVHN and 78.77% on CIFAR-10.
